Our services

Care that fits around your life

Every service can be booked on its own or combined into one plan. Visits are available weekdays, evenings and weekends.

Personal careDomesticMealsTransportCompanionshipRespiteAfter hospitalLive-in careDementia careCare Concierge

Personal care

Help with the most personal parts of the day, given with patience and dignity. Your carer follows your routine and your preferences, every time.

  • Showering, bathing and toileting
  • Dressing and undressing
  • Hair, shaving and grooming
  • Continence support
  • Medication prompts and reminders
  • Help getting in and out of bed

Often a good fit for people who are finding mornings or evenings harder, or whose family can't be there every day.

Domestic assistance

A clean, safe home without the bending, lifting and stretching that cause falls.

  • Vacuuming, mopping and dusting
  • Bathroom and kitchen cleaning
  • Laundry, ironing and linen changes
  • Bins out and back
  • Tidying and decluttering walkways
  • Light pet care

Often a good fit for anyone who manages personal care well but finds housework tiring or risky.

Meals and shopping

Good food keeps people well. We cook familiar favourites and work around any dietary needs you tell us about.

  • Breakfast, lunch or dinner preparation
  • Batch cooking for the week
  • Grocery shopping, with you or for you
  • Checking the fridge for out-of-date food
  • Support with eating and drinking
  • Writing shopping lists together

Often a good fit for people living alone, or anyone who has started skipping meals.

Transport and outings

We drive, walk alongside and wait with you, so appointments and errands stop being a worry.

  • GP, specialist and hospital appointments
  • Pharmacy, bank and post office
  • Hairdresser and podiatrist
  • Church, clubs and community groups
  • Visiting family and friends
  • Coffee, lunch and scenic drives

Often a good fit for people who no longer drive, or who feel unsteady going out alone.

Companionship

Loneliness affects health as much as any illness. A regular visit from someone friendly can change a whole week.

  • Conversation over a cuppa
  • Short walks and gentle exercise
  • Gardening, cards, puzzles and crafts
  • Reading and letter writing
  • Help with phones, tablets and video calls
  • Photo albums and life stories

Often a good fit for people who live alone or have recently lost a partner.

Respite and overnight care

Family carers need rest too. We step in for a few hours, a full day, or overnight, so you can recharge knowing your loved one is in good hands.

  • Daytime in-home respite
  • Overnight active or sleepover support
  • Weekend cover
  • Holiday cover while family is away
  • Emergency respite when we can
  • Detailed handover notes

Often a good fit for partners and adult children who are the main carer.

Support after hospital

The first weeks home from hospital are when people are most at risk. Short-term extra support helps recovery stay on track.

  • Help settling in on discharge day
  • Daily check-ins for the first weeks
  • Personal care while mobility returns
  • Meals and shopping
  • Transport to follow-up appointments
  • Reducing visits as you recover

Often a good fit for anyone coming home after surgery, a fall or an illness.

Live-in and 24-hour care

The alternative to moving into an aged care home. A dedicated carer lives in, with a second carer rotating in for breaks, so your loved one stays in the home and neighbourhood they know.

  • Two rotating live-in carers
  • Up to 8 hours of hands-on care a day
  • Personal care, meals and household
  • Outings, routines and hobbies kept up
  • Care manager visits and family updates
  • After-hours line for urgent matters

Often a good fit for people with high care needs who want to stay at home, and families weighing up residential care.

Dementia and memory care

People living with dementia do best with familiar faces and steady routines. We keep the same small team and build each day around what still brings joy.

  • Carers with dementia training
  • Consistent daily routines
  • Safe, calm home set-up
  • Meaningful activities and music
  • Support with sundowning evenings
  • Guidance and respite for family

Often a good fit for families noticing memory changes, or caring for someone with a dementia diagnosis.

Care Concierge

The extra help that makes life run smoothly, booked by the hour at Lotus Essentials rates and arranged by your care manager.

  • Hospital and specialist chaperone
  • Travel companion, local or interstate
  • Event and family occasion companion
  • Tradespeople and deliveries let in
  • Home ready before you return
  • Appointments booked and tracked

Often a good fit for busy families, and clients who want a trusted person by their side.

Getting started

Starting care, step by step

Most families have a home visit within 2 business days and care in place within about a week. Here's exactly what happens and what it costs.

  1. Get in touch

    Call us or send an enquiry. We'll ask a few questions about current needs, health and home, and book a time to visit.

  2. Free home visit

    Usually within 2 business days, a 45 to 60 minute visit at home with your care manager. We meet the person, talk with family, and look at home safety together.

  3. Your care plan and quote

    Within two business days you receive a written plan: tasks, visit days and times, your carer, and the total cost.

  4. Care begins

    We introduce your carer in person, then call after the first week to adjust anything that isn't quite right.

Starting care

Can I see what care might look like before I call?

Yes. Our care plan builder shows a sample week, a sample visit and an estimated weekly cost in about a minute. You can send it with your enquiry, and your care manager will use it as a starting point at your free home visit.

How quickly can care start?

We usually visit within 2 business days of your first call and start care within about a week. For hospital discharges, tell us the discharge date as early as you can and we'll aim to have care ready on the day.

Do I need a referral or assessment?

No. Because Gigi Care provides private care, you can contact us directly. Our free home visit is all we need to write your plan.

Can family members be involved?

Yes, and we encourage it. Family can join the home visit, receive visit notes, and call the office during business hours, with the client's permission.

What areas do you cover?

All of metropolitan Melbourne, the Mornington Peninsula, Phillip Island and regional Victoria. See the Areas page, or call us with your suburb or town and we’ll let you know straight away.

Costs and payment

How much does home care cost?

Weekday care starts from $109 per hour, or $106 when you book 10 or more hours a week. Live-in care starts from $985 per day, or $7,255 for a full week. All prices include GST. Weekend, night and public holiday rates are in our price guide on the Getting started page.

Can I use a government home care budget?

Not with Gigi Care yet. We currently provide private, self-funded care. If you receive government-funded support through another provider, we can add private hours alongside it.

Is there a minimum booking?

Visits are a minimum of 2 hours, or 3 hours on public holidays. Lotus Signature needs 10 or more hours a week, active nights 8 hours, sleepovers 1 night, live-in care 3 days in a row, and nursing visits 1 hour.

Are there any contracts or exit fees?

No lock-in contracts and no exit fees. You can change or stop services at any time.

How do I cancel or change a visit?

Call the office. Regular visits changed with at least 24 hours' notice are free; with less notice, 50% of the visit is charged, and the full visit if the carer arrives and it can't go ahead. Active nights, sleepovers and live-in care need 7 days' notice, or the first night or day is charged. If your loved one goes into hospital, tell us before the visit starts and it won't be charged.

Our carers

What does a care manager do?

Your care manager is your family’s single point of contact. They run your home visit, write and review the care plan, match your carer, visit regularly and answer your calls.

How does live-in care work?

Two carers rotate, each living in for a set number of days, so support continues day and night without one person burning out. Each day includes up to 8 hours of hands-on care, with the carer close by the rest of the time. Your home needs a private bedroom for the carer, and the carer shares meals with the household. Your care manager plans the roster and visits regularly.

Will I have the same carer each time?

We aim for a small, regular team for each client. If your usual carer is away, we'll tell you in advance who is coming.

How are carers screened?

Every carer has a current national police check, relevant qualifications, First Aid and CPR, and reference checks before their first shift.

Can I ask for a different carer?

Of course. Good matches matter. Just let the office know, no explanation needed.

Safety and concerns

What happens if a carer notices a problem?

Carers report changes in health, mood or home safety to the office the same day. We contact the client and, with permission, family or the GP.

How do I make a complaint?

Call or email the office, or use the contact form. We acknowledge every complaint within two business days. See our Policies page for the full process.

Privacy policy

Your privacy matters

How we collect, use, share and protect personal and health information. Version 1.0 · November 2026.

Precious Solace Pty Ltd trading as Gigi Care Australia (ABN 28 691 586 099) (Gigi Care, we, us) provides private home care across Victoria. Because we provide health services and hold health information, we are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic), including the Health Privacy Principles (HPPs).

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use, share and protect it, and how you can access it, correct it or make a complaint.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for our services and business. Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

  • Clients: name, date of birth, address, contact details, next of kin and representatives, decision-maker or attorney details, GP and pharmacy details, health conditions, medications, allergies, care needs and preferences, cultural and language background, home safety information, key safe codes, visit notes, incident records, photos taken with consent, and billing and payment details.
  • Families and representatives: name, relationship to the client, contact details and, for account holders, billing details.
  • Job applicants and staff: contact details, work history, qualifications, references, right to work, police check results, driver’s licence and insurance, health and vaccination information relevant to the role, and bank, tax and superannuation details.
  • Website visitors and enquirers: details you enter in our forms, and basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited.
  • Suppliers and referrers: business contact and payment details.

2. How we collect it

  • Directly from you, in person, by phone, email, our website forms, intake and assessment forms, and our care app.
  • From others with your consent or where the law allows, such as family members, representatives, your GP, hospital discharge teams, allied health professionals and referrers.
  • From our carers and care managers, who record visit notes and observations while providing care.
  • For job applicants, from referees, police check providers and qualification issuers, with your consent.

Where it’s lawful and practicable, you may deal with us anonymously or using a pseudonym, for example when making a general enquiry. We usually can’t provide care without identifying you.

3. Why we collect and use it

  • To assess your needs, write and review your care plan, and provide safe, coordinated care
  • To match and roster carers, and communicate with you, your family and representatives
  • To respond to emergencies, incidents and changes in health
  • To invoice and receive payment
  • To recruit, screen, train, employ and pay our staff
  • To manage complaints, feedback, quality, safety, insurance and legal claims
  • To meet our legal obligations, including workplace safety, tax, employment and record-keeping laws
  • With your consent, to send you news and offers. You can opt out at any time, and we never sell your information.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information for the purpose it was collected, a related purpose you would reasonably expect, with your consent, or where the law requires or permits. This may include:

  • Carers, nurses and care managers who support you
  • Family members and representatives you nominate
  • Your GP, pharmacist, hospital and other health professionals involved in your care
  • Emergency services, where there is a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety
  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as our care management software, phone, IT, payment, accounting, insurance and legal providers, under confidentiality obligations
  • Government agencies, regulators, courts or police, where required or authorised by law

5. Overseas disclosure

We store information in Australia wherever possible. Some of our service providers, such as email, cloud software and IT support providers, may store or access information in other countries, including the United States. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the provider protects the information in line with the APPs.

6. How we protect it

  • Client records are kept in secure, password-protected systems with access limited to staff who need it
  • Staff are trained in privacy and confidentiality, and sign confidentiality obligations
  • Our care app is protected by passcodes and can be locked remotely if a device is lost
  • Paper records are kept to a minimum and stored securely
  • Key safe codes are shared only with rostered carers

7. How long we keep it

We keep health records for at least 7 years after your last service, or for a person who was under 18 when information was collected, until they turn 25, as required by the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic). Other records are kept as required by law. When information is no longer needed, we securely destroy or de-identify it.

8. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify the affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

9. Our website

Our website may use cookies and analytics to understand how it is used and improve it. This information doesn’t identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Our website may link to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see, or have a copy of, the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. Contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. We don’t charge for requests, but may charge a reasonable fee for copying large records. If we refuse a request, we will explain why in writing and how you can complain.

11. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about privacy, please contact our Privacy Officer. We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days and aim to respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact:

  • Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: 1300 363 992, oaic.gov.au
  • Health Complaints Commissioner (Victoria): 1300 582 113, hcc.vic.gov.au, for health information

12. Contact our Privacy Officer

  • Email: hello@gigicare.com.au
  • Phone: (03) 5910 9999

13. Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least once a year and may update it. The current version is always on our website, and a copy is available free on request.

      Terms of service

      Clear terms, written in plain English

      These terms apply to all private care services from Precious Solace Pty Ltd (ABN 28 691 586 099), trading as Gigi Care Australia. Your signed service agreement and care plan set out the details for your family.

      Your service agreement

      Before care begins, you receive a written service agreement and care plan listing the services, visit times, your carer, and every rate that applies. Nothing is charged until you have signed it.

      Prices

      • Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Registered nurse services are GST-free. If the GST rate changes, prices change by the same amount.
      • Rates apply to the time of day the care is delivered, as shown on our Getting started page.
      • Minimum bookings: 2 hours per visit (3 on public holidays), 8 hours for active nights, 1 night for sleepovers, 3 days in a row for live-in care, and 1 hour for nursing.
      • Kilometres driven with you during a visit are charged at $2.00 per km. Parking, tolls, entry tickets and a travel companion's fares, accommodation and meals are charged at cost.
      • Services we arrange from others, such as gardening or meal delivery, are charged at cost plus 10%. Extra care coordination beyond the included reviews is $132 per hour.
      • Sleepovers include up to 3 hours of care, and live-in care up to 8 hours a day. Care beyond this is charged at the hourly rate. If care is regularly needed during the night, we'll talk with you about moving to active night care.
      • Carer travel to and from your home is free within 25 km. Where the nearest available carer is further away, mostly in regional Victoria, a travel fee of $27.50 per visit applies and is shown in your quote.
      • Lotus Signature rates apply when your booked hours average 10 or more a week over each fortnight.
      • Active nights have an 8-hour minimum. Sleepovers and live-in care need a private bed or bedroom for the carer, and live-in care also needs meals for the carer.
      • We review prices each 1 July, when award wages change, and give you at least 30 days' written notice of any change. If you don't accept a change, you can end services before it takes effect, with no exit fee.

      Invoices and payment

      • Regular care is invoiced fortnightly in advance for your scheduled visits, and is due within 7 days. Any changes are adjusted on the next invoice.
      • Live-in care is invoiced weekly in advance.
      • Direct debit and bank transfer are free. Card payments carry a small surcharge that covers our card fees, shown before you pay.
      • If an invoice is more than 14 days overdue, we may pause services after contacting you.

      Special offers

      • Welcome offer: new clients who complete their first 4 weeks of regular care, with 6 or more hours a week, receive 2 weekday hours of care free, credited on the next invoice.
      • Pay ahead and save: 3% off Lotus Essentials hourly care when 4 weeks are paid in advance by bank transfer or direct debit.
      • Lotus Residence 4-week stay: 5% off live-in care when 4 weeks are booked and paid in advance.
      • 6-month commitment: 4 weekday Care Concierge hours, used within the 6 months, when you commit to 6 months of Lotus Signature. If you end services early, used concierge hours are charged at the standard rate.
      • Refer a family: $150 credit for you and the family you refer, applied after they complete 4 weeks of care. Credits have no cash value.
      • Offers can't be combined, except the referral credit. If you end services before a prepaid period finishes, the care you received is recalculated at standard rates and the rest is refunded. Concierge hours included in packages are weekday hours and don't carry over to the next month. We may change or end offers for new bookings at any time.

      Changes and cancellations

      • Regular visits changed or cancelled with at least 24 hours' notice are free.
      • With less than 24 hours' notice, 50% of the visit is charged.
      • If the carer arrives and the visit can't go ahead, the full visit is charged.
      • Active nights, sleepovers and live-in care need 7 days' notice to cancel or reduce. With less notice, the first night or day is charged.
      • If you go into hospital, visits are not charged when you tell us before the visit starts.
      • If a visit runs past the booked time at your request, the extra time is charged in 15-minute blocks.

      Ending services

      There are no lock-in contracts and no exit fees. You can end regular services at any time with 7 days' notice, or 14 days' notice for live-in care. We may end services with 14 days' written notice, for example if we can no longer safely meet your needs, and will help you find another provider where we can. We may pause or end services immediately if there is a serious risk to the safety of staff, abuse or harassment of staff, or unpaid invoices after notice. Where it's safe to do so, we'll talk it through with you first.

      A safe workplace for carers

      • Please don't smoke during visits, and keep pets secured if your carer asks.
      • Carers can't accept money, valuable gifts, loans or inclusion in a will.
      • Please arrange all care through Gigi Care rather than directly with a carer, so every visit stays insured and supervised.
